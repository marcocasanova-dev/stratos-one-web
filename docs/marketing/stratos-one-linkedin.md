## 3. About

Stratos One builds AI agents and software integrations that help businesses handle enquiries, collect information and coordinate follow-up.

We combine AI automation with experienced human engineering—from understanding the workflow to connecting systems, testing behaviour and maintaining the solution.

Our starting focus is enquiry handling: turning incoming messages into structured, actionable requests so teams can spend more time helping customers.

Services include:
- Workflow assessments and automation planning.
- AI agent pilots and implementation.
- Business system and API integrations.
- Monitoring, maintenance and ongoing improvements.

Each workflow has defined permissions and clear human handoffs. Routine work can move automatically, while exceptions reach the people responsible for making decisions.

Stratos One is founded and led by Marco Casanova, a software engineer with more than 15 years of experience across industrial IoT, healthcare, e-commerce and enterprise applications.

Have a workflow that takes too much manual coordination? Let's explore what could be improved.

https://stratos-one.eu/en/

## 8. Owner launch sequence (not automated)

1. Review and approve the website changes.
2. Confirm operator information, the working contact destination and missing legal details.
3. Publish the approved website through the normal project workflow.
4. Create or update the LinkedIn company page using this pack.
5. Connect the real company page URL to the website and Marco's profile.
6. Publish the launch post once the site matches the offer.
7. Speak with 10–15 prospective businesses to validate the workflow and audience.
8. Seek a bounded paid pilot; measure outcomes before claiming savings.
9. Aim for three paying customers with substantially similar needs before investing in a generalized SaaS platform.

Useful pilot measures: time spent handling enquiries, completeness of collected
information, handoff quality, exception rate, operating cost and actual support
effort. Agree targets from a real baseline.
